01 / CONTEXT

Why this product exists

Doctor discovery is trust-sensitive. Patients need to compare profiles, understand specialties, and see useful information without navigating a confusing booking funnel.

Who it is for

Patients searching for a doctor by specialty or need who want enough information to make a confident choice before booking an appointment.

The design challenge

MyDoct is a healthcare discovery and booking concept that helps patients find doctors, review profiles, and move toward an appointment. The interface brings doctor information, availability, and patient-oriented decision support into a cohesive mobile experience.

02 / PROBLEM

What needed to change

The design work starts by turning broad business friction into a small set of user-facing problems that can be prioritized and solved.

Problem statement

  • Patients may not know which provider is the right fit.
  • Doctor profiles can become information-heavy.
  • Booking needs to remain simple after a longer discovery step.
  • Trust signals need to be visible without overwhelming the user.

Design hypothesis

Build discovery around clear provider profiles, meaningful specialties, availability, and focused booking actions. The design uses cards and profile detail to let users compare first and commit second.

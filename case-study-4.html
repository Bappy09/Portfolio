01 / CONTEXT

Why this product exists

Real-estate websites often overload users with listings and specifications. Sonder House needed a more focused story that creates desire while keeping property discovery and conversion paths obvious.

Who it is for

Prospective buyers and renters exploring modern residential properties, especially users who want to understand the feeling, features, and practical details of a place before taking the next step.

The design challenge

Sonder House is a modern real-estate web experience built around property discovery and the emotional appeal of contemporary living. The design uses editorial composition, large imagery, and clear calls to action to make the property story feel premium without becoming difficult to navigate.

02 / PROBLEM

What needed to change

The design work starts by turning broad business friction into a small set of user-facing problems that can be prioritized and solved.

Problem statement

  • Property value can be lost in generic listing layouts.
  • Users need both emotional and practical information.
  • Large imagery can dominate if hierarchy is not controlled.
  • The path from discovery to inquiry should remain obvious.

Design hypothesis

Use an editorial landing experience that combines cinematic property imagery with concise copy, strong section hierarchy, and focused calls to action. The interface tells the story of the home first, then supports exploration with practical detail.

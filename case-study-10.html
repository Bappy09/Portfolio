Compare Smarter. Move With Confidence.

My Good Movers is a moving-company discovery and comparison platform designed to help people find reliable interstate and long-distance movers with greater confidence.

The platform brings together:

  • Licensed moving companies
  • Verified customer reviews
  • Moving prices
  • Ratings
  • Services
  • Interstate routes
  • Moving guides
  • Cost estimation
  • Mover comparison
  • Free moving estimates

into one centralized experience.

The core idea is simple:

Choosing a moving company shouldn't feel like gambling with your belongings.

My Good Movers helps users research, compare, verify, and ultimately choose a mover before committing to a move.

The platform's current website explicitly positions itself around comparing trusted movers, verified reviews, pricing, and services in one place.

Why My Good Movers Exists

Moving to another city or state is a major life event.

For most people, choosing the moving company is not something they do regularly.

That creates a major information problem.

A user may find dozens or hundreds of moving companies online, but the real questions are:

Which companies are actually licensed?

Which ones have real customer reviews?

How much should the move cost?

Which company is reliable for my specific route?

Who should I trust with my belongings?

The problem is therefore not simply:

“Find a mover.”

The real problem is:

“Find the right mover with enough confidence to make a high-stakes decision.”

My Good Movers exists to simplify that decision.

The Business Problem

The core business challenge was to transform an overwhelming moving-company search into a trustworthy comparison experience.

Several problems had to be addressed.

01 — Too Many Moving Companies

Consumers can encounter a large number of moving companies when searching online.

More choices do not automatically create better decisions.

Without structured comparison, users can become overwhelmed.

02 — Trust Is Difficult to Establish

A moving company will physically handle a customer's belongings and potentially transport them hundreds or thousands of miles.

Users need confidence before making that decision.

MGM therefore places significant emphasis on:

  • Verified reviews
  • Licensing
  • Ratings
  • Pricing
  • Methodology
  • Company information

The platform's own methodology explains that its evaluations consider customer feedback, experience, services, pricing, credentials, and ongoing monitoring.

03 — Moving Prices Are Unclear

Moving costs can vary substantially depending on:

  • Distance
  • Move size
  • Route
  • Timing
  • Services
  • Company

This makes it difficult for consumers to know whether a quote is reasonable.

MGM addresses this by surfacing price information and providing moving-cost tools.

04 — Reviews Can Be Hard to Trust

A star rating alone is not enough.

Users want to know:

  • Was the reviewer actually a customer?
  • What kind of move did they complete?
  • How much did the move cost?
  • What did they experience?

MGM makes verified reviews a central part of the product experience.

05 — Users Need More Than a Company List

A directory alone does not solve the decision problem.

Users also need:

Comparison + Reviews + Pricing + Routes + Guides + Estimates

The product therefore needs to work as a decision-support platform rather than simply a directory.

Primary Audience — Long-Distance Movers

People preparing to move from one city or state to another.

This is the core audience because interstate moving involves greater distance, cost, and uncertainty than a simple local move.

First-Time Long-Distance Movers

People who have little experience choosing a moving company.

They need:

  • Education
  • Guidance
  • Trust signals
  • Pricing context
  • Simple comparison

Families Relocating

Families moving homes need to protect a large amount of personal property while managing a major life transition.

They are likely to value:

  • Reliability
  • Reviews
  • Price transparency
  • Company reputation
  • Route coverage

Professionals & Relocating Individuals

People relocating for:

  • Jobs
  • Career opportunities
  • Lifestyle changes
  • Housing changes

They often need a fast way to compare movers without spending hours researching individual companies.

Price-Conscious Users

Users who want to compare prices before requesting a quote.

For these users, cost information and moving calculators are important decision-making tools.

01 — “I Don't Know Which Mover to Trust.”

The biggest emotional barrier is uncertainty.

02 — “There Are Too Many Options.”

Searching independently can produce a huge list of companies without meaningful prioritization.

03 — “I Don't Know What a Fair Price Is.”

Without benchmarks, users may struggle to evaluate quotes.

04 — “Can I Trust These Reviews?”

Reviews need credibility and context.

05 — “Is This Company Right for My Route?”

A mover may be highly rated but not necessarily the right fit for a specific interstate route.

06 — “I Don't Know What I Should Look For.”

First-time movers may not understand:

  • Licensing
  • Pricing models
  • Services
  • Moving terminology
  • Red flags

The platform therefore needs to educate as well as compare.

The experience was designed around six major objectives:

1. Build trust immediately

2. Make moving-company comparison simple

3. Reduce decision anxiety

4. Make reviews and pricing understandable

5. Guide users toward the right mover

6. Convert research intent into quote requests

The central UX strategy can be summarized as:

Trust → Understand → Compare → Verify → Act

This is the foundation of the entire experience.

A 5-star review by itself tells very little.

Visual Design Strategy

The visual design is intentionally calm and trustworthy.

It uses:

  • Light blue backgrounds
  • Deep navy
  • White cards
  • Blue CTA accents
  • Large typography
  • Rounded containers
  • Soft shadows
  • Real photography
  • Spacious layouts
  • High-contrast dark methodology section

Why Blue Works Here

Blue communicates:

  • Trust
  • Reliability
  • Safety
  • Professionalism
  • Stability

These are exactly the emotional qualities required for a moving decision.

The product is asking users to trust someone with their belongings.

Therefore, the visual language avoids aggressive or overly playful branding.

Real Photography

The hero uses a real-life moving context:

A person sitting among moving boxes and preparing for a relocation.

This immediately communicates:

Moving is a real life event.

The image also humanizes the product.

Instead of showing a truck alone, the design shows the person experiencing the move.

This keeps the user at the center.

Light vs Dark

The website uses a light interface for:

  • Search
  • Statistics
  • Reviews
  • Guides
  • Location discovery

and a dark section for:

  • Methodology
  • Safety
  • Verification

This creates a visual distinction between:

Explore

and:

Understand why you can trust us.

The dark methodology section feels more serious and authoritative.

That is appropriate for safety and verification content.

Card-Based Information Architecture

Cards are used for:

  • Trust principles
  • Reviews
  • Guides
  • Statistics
  • Moving routes

This allows complex information to be broken into digestible units.

Each card communicates one idea.

That supports fast scanning.

What I Focused on as a Product Designer

The central challenge was not simply designing a moving website.

It was designing a decision-making experience for a high-stakes purchase.

I focused on:

Trust

Making verification, reviews, ratings, and methodology visible.

Comparison

Helping users compare movers rather than researching them individually.

Price Transparency

Showing average costs and connecting users with estimates.

Geographic Relevance

Organizing content around states and moving routes.

Education

Providing guides and tools for users who are still researching.

Conversion

Creating multiple paths toward comparison, estimates, and support.

Design Outcome

The final experience transforms a complicated moving-company search into a guided decision journey.

Instead of:

Search Google → Open many websites → Read random reviews → Request quotes → Compare manually

the experience becomes:

Enter Route → Discover Movers → Compare → Read Verified Reviews → Understand Pricing → Get Estimate → Choose

This dramatically simplifies the user's research process.

The Core Product Value

My Good Movers is essentially solving an information asymmetry problem.

Moving companies know their services, pricing, credentials, and reputation.

The consumer does not.

MGM acts as an information layer between them.

It gives the consumer:

Data + Reviews + Pricing + Verification + Comparison

so they can make a more informed decision.

Key UX Decisions

From Directory → Decision Platform

The product does not simply list moving companies.

It helps users decide.

From “Best” → “Best Fit”

Instead of assuming one company is best for everyone, the experience considers the user's route and needs.

From Star Rating → Verified Evidence

Reviews include context and verification instead of functioning as simple decorative ratings.

From “Trust Us” → “Here's Our Methodology”

The methodology section explains how movers, pricing, and reviews are evaluated.

From Generic Search → Route-Based Discovery

Origin and destination are introduced immediately because moving decisions are geographic.

From One Conversion → Multiple Paths

Users can:

Compare Movers

Get an Estimate

Read Guides

Explore Routes

Call

This accommodates different levels of purchase readiness.

From Blog → Decision Support

Guides and calculators are integrated into the product journey rather than treated as unrelated content.

Key Takeaways

Trust is the actual product experience.

For a high-risk service such as interstate moving, trust cannot be a footer element.

It must appear throughout the journey.

Comparison reduces decision fatigue.

Users should not have to research dozens of companies independently.

Verified reviews are more powerful when contextualized.

A review becomes significantly more useful when users can understand the real moving experience behind it.

Transparency creates credibility.

Explaining methodology is often more convincing than simply claiming to be trustworthy.

Location is a core part of the UX.

Moving companies should be evaluated in the context of the user's actual route.

Content can support conversion.

Guides, calculators, and educational resources help users move from uncertainty toward action.

The best marketplace UX doesn't just provide more choices.

It helps users make better choices.
